## Make it yours

- **Change the look:** try another pattern from the [catalog](/docs/catalog), a [colour](/docs/look/color-and-theme), or a [material](/docs/look/materials) such as glow.
- **Connect your app:** drive it with [values](/docs/connect/values), such as a ring's `progress`, or follow a [voice agent](/docs/connect/voice).
- **Follow your app's flow:** switch looks with [states](/docs/connect/states-vs-patterns), all described in one [FX Spec](/docs/reference/fx-spec) file.
