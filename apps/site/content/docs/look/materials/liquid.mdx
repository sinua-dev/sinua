Liquid treats every element as a soft field and draws the contour where the fields merge: filled blobs, outlines, or dots resampled along the contour. Nearby dots flow together into droplets and pull apart as the pattern moves.

## Cost and low power

Liquid is compute-heavy (the contour is traced every frame) while drawing few elements, so the cost badge can under-rate it. Measure on your target devices ([Performance](/docs/performance)). Sheddable in low power (`disable: ["liquid"]`).

Some patterns suit it better than others; each pattern gets its own tuned liquid defaults, listed below the table.
