title: Gradient
description: A colour ramp by position across the frame.
---

A gradient colours elements by where they sit: a sweep from one hue to another (or three) across the frame, and along each stroke on rings, beacons and signal traces. The full guide is on [Colour & theme](/docs/look/color-and-theme#a-gradient).

<Demo object="ring" pattern="tracking" />

## Cost and low power

No extra elements. Sheddable in low power (`disable: ["gradient"]`), which falls back to the pattern's plain colour.
