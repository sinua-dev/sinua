## When to use which pattern

| Pattern | `pattern` | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| [Completing](#completing) | `completing` | One task's progress: an arc that fills. |
| [Loading](#loading) | `loading` | Indeterminate wait: a spinner. |
| [Tracking](#tracking) | `tracking` | Several goals at once: nested activity rings with extra laps. |
| [Stepping](#stepping) | `stepping` | Discrete steps: segments that fill one by one. |
| [Measuring](#measuring) | `measuring` | A reading within a range: a gauge. |
| [Talking](#talking) | `talking` | Who is talking: a ring round a participant's picture. |

## Who is talking

`talking` is a ring round a person's picture, for a call or a group conversation. It thickens outward with their voice and never covers the face; ripples go out while they speak and in while they listen, and an arc circles while they think. `SinuaAvatar` puts the image in the middle for you (not on React Native yet).
