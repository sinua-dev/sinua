---
title: Pulse
description: A periodic breathe of opacity and scale on any pattern.
---

Pulse makes any pattern breathe: it dips opacity and scale once per `period` seconds. Use it for a gentle "waiting" feel on patterns that are otherwise still, or bind `pulse.strength` to a value so the breathing deepens as it rises.

## Cost and low power

Free to draw: it scales and fades what's already there. Sheddable in low power (`disable: ["pulse"]`).

The period is in real seconds on every pattern, whatever the pattern's own speed.
