Noise moves every point a little along a slow, smooth field, so geometric patterns feel alive and hand-made. Points that are shared (a ring's stroke, a network's links) stay attached.

## Cost and low power

Cheap: it moves existing elements and adds none. Sheddable in low power (`disable: ["noise"]`).

Keep `amplitude` small (a few percent of the size): noise reads as life at low amounts and as a glitch at high ones.
