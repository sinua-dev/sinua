A voice source can report what both speakers say as they say it, so you can put captions under the visual. It's a data layer only: Sinua draws no captions. **Display only: Sinua keeps nothing beyond the current turn's text and sends it nowhere.**

- **Where:** `onTranscript` on a source or on `SharedVoiceSource` (Web; it returns its unsubscribe), `listenTranscript` on `SharedVoiceSource` (iOS, Android), and `onTranscript` on a React Native voice handle. Subscribing before `connect()` catches the first turn.
- **Callbacks** arrive on the main actor (iOS), the Main dispatcher (Android) or the event loop (Web), like every other callback.

## The update

Every update has the same shape on every platform and vendor: `{ role, text, final, turnId, truncated?, startMs?, endMs? }`.

- **`text` is the whole turn so far**, never a diff: replace what you show, don't append.
- **`turnId`** (`"u3"`, `"a4"`) stays the same for the whole turn and keeps counting for the source's lifetime, reconnects included.
- **`final: true`** comes exactly once per turn, and nothing follows it.
- **Both speakers at once.** User and assistant turns are independent, so both can grow at the same time.
- **Barge-in.** When the user cuts the assistant off, its turn ends with one update, `truncated: true` and `final: true`, whose `text` is the part that was actually played. An unspoken word doesn't flash up.
- **When a turn ends.** An assistant turn ends when the source stops speaking, after its short quiet tail, so a natural pause doesn't split it. A "let me check" followed by the answer gives two assistant turns.
- Text is NFC-normalized and otherwise left as the vendor sent it.

## Synced or raw

By default the assistant's text appears **with the played audio**, word by word. With `syncToAudio: false` (Web option, iOS init, Android constructor) it appears as soon as the vendor sends it, with the vendor's own timing in `startMs` / `endMs`. The user's text shows as it arrives in both modes.

How close the synced text follows the audio on GPT-Live, measured against word timings (which carry ±100–200 ms of their own):

| Mode | Turkish | English |
|---|---|---|
| synced (default) | median 82 ms, p95 191 ms | median ~190 ms, p95 ~515 ms |
| raw (`syncToAudio: false`) | text ~650 ms ahead of the audio | text ~690 ms ahead |

GPT-Live's word timings are approximate and how far they run ahead varies from session to session, so treat the English figures as a known limit. `syncToAudio` also exists on ElevenLabs, Gemini Live and OpenAI Realtime.

## Which sources send transcripts

`supportsTranscript` says whether a source sends them, and `transcriptTiming` how it times the text.

| Source | `transcriptTiming` | How the assistant's text follows the audio |
|---|---|---|
| [GPT-Live](/docs/connect/voice/openai-live) (`OpenAILiveVoiceSource`) | `segments` | GPT-Live's word timings, anchored at each turn's audio onset |
| [ElevenLabs](/docs/connect/voice/elevenlabs) | `chars` | ElevenLabs' per-character times against the played audio: character-exact |
| [Gemini Live](/docs/connect/voice/gemini-live) | `none` | Gemini sends no times: the text is paced over the reply's audio |
| [OpenAI Realtime](/docs/connect/voice/openai-realtime) | `none` | Realtime sends no times: paced at about 14 characters a second while the model is audible, caught up when it stops |
| [LiveKit](/docs/connect/voice/livekit) | `synced` | the agent's transcription stream, which LiveKit Agents already syncs to its speech and cuts on a barge-in |
| [Simulated](/docs/connect/voice/simulated) (`SimulatedVoiceSource`) | `synced` | the script's line as it is said |
| mic, test tone | — | `supportsTranscript` is false |

- **Realtime** needs the session's input transcription for the user's side, which OpenAI bills per minute. When something subscribes and the session has none, the source turns it on with `gpt-4o-mini-transcribe`; `transcribeUser` picks another model, or `false` / `nil` / `null` leaves the session as your backend made it. Without it, only the assistant's side comes.
- **Gemini** asks for output (and input) transcription in the Live setup. Its user transcript can come late, or not at all, on long speech.
- **LiveKit:** a Room takes one handler per text-stream topic. If your app already reads `lk.transcription` on a Room you attached, the source's transcript stays empty (with a console warning); read your own handler instead. Raw mode is the agent's setting there, not the source's.
- **Raw mode** shows the text as it arrives on every vendor. At a barge-in, `chars` keeps the characters played by then and `none` the text that had arrived.
- Only GPT-Live has been measured on live sessions; the others' timing follows from what each vendor sends.

For your own voice source, `@sinua/voice` exports `TranscriptAssembler`: feed it your vendor's events and it gives the same updates.
