Particles lift off the pattern: they drift out, flow in, orbit or rise, then fade. Each pattern has its own default behaviour: ring patterns rise gently, scanning gathers particles in, speaking follows the voice.

## Cost and low power

Particles are ordinary dots, so they count like any other element. Sheddable in low power (`disable: ["particles"]`), and off by default in low power when your spec says nothing.

`life` is in real seconds. `sync: 1` turns a steady stream into one burst per life; `audio` couples brightness to the voice level.
