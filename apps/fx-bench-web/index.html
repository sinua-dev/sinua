sinua bench

Runs every case in spec/bench/cases.json twice (normal, low power) and reports frame, compute and paint times. Keep this tab in the foreground and don't touch the page while it runs. Desktop-browser numbers are not device numbers: run it on the phone for those (docs/bench.md).
