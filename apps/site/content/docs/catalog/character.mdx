A character is a face for your voice assistant. The gaze knows whose turn it is: it looks at you while you talk, looks up and away while it thinks, and blinks when your turn ends. The mouth follows only how loud the agent's voice is. Give it a voice source or an agent state, like every other object.

## When to use which pattern

| Pattern | `pattern` | Who it is | How it shows the voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Buzzy](#buzzy) | `buzzy` | A small space-hero assistant: a rounded helmet, a glass visor over a face screen, a voice core in the chest. | The mouth is a voice line: two even waves that flow, and the voice's level sets only their height, so it never jitters. While listening, sound arcs light at its ear pods with your voice. |
| [Hum](#hum) | `hum` | A vintage studio microphone that hosts the show, with its face on the grille. | The grille's slots light with the voice. A tally light is red while listening and blinks amber while thinking. |
| [Wisp](#wisp) | `wisp` | A helpful spirit: a glowing head flowing into a curling smoke tail. | Its sparkles gather in while it listens, circle its head while it thinks and stream out with its voice. |
| [Chirp](#chirp) | `chirp` | A songbird with teal wings and a feather crest. | The beak opens with the voice and little notes rise from it. While thinking, three thought dots light in turn. |
| [Cuppa](#cuppa) | `cuppa` | A coffee mug on its saucer. | Steam rises while it listens and thickens with its voice. |
| [Bean](#bean) | `bean` | A coffee bean on little feet, with aroma sparkles. | Its sparkles gather while it listens, circle while it thinks and stream out with its voice. |
| [Beep](#beep) | `beep` | A small tin robot with arms. | A hand goes to its ear to listen and to its chin to think, and its arms beat with the voice. `arms: false` takes them off. |

## How it behaves

- **The eyes are shapes.** No pupils and no brows: the eye's shape is the expression, so it still reads at 20 px.
- **It knows whose turn it is.** It looks at you while listening, looks up and away while thinking, and blinks once as your turn ends.
- **The mouth follows the voice's loudness.** Nothing more: no lip-sync, so any voice source works.
- **Effects are expressions.** [`success`](/docs/connect/effects) is `^ ^` eyes with a grin, `error` is `x x` eyes with a short shake, `celebrate` is star eyes with a burst.
- **Muted, it squints and fades.** Its colours stay.
- **State changes are smooth.** The eyes slide up as thinking starts; they don't jump.

## Head turn

The head turns like a solid, not a flat sticker: the face slides round its surface and the body shows the turn. At rest it looks from corner to corner, listening it turns to you, thinking it looks up and away, and speaking it nods with the voice.

`turn` sets how far: the default is about ±25°, `1` is ±35° and `0` draws it flat. At 20 px the head doesn't turn, and with reduced motion only the facing of each state stays. It stays in the [light cost class](/docs/performance).

## Expressions

`expression` gives the character a mood your app picks. It stays until you change it, and a change eases over 0.6 s.

| `expression` | Eyes | Resting mouth |
|---|---|---|
| `happy` | the happy arc | a smile |
| `surprised` | wider and taller, lids open | a small "O" |
| `thoughtful` | a lid, the left eye shorter, a slight tilt | a smile |
| `sad` | a lid, outer corners dropped | a downturned line |
| `sleepy` | heavy lids, a slow deep breath | a smile |
| `none` | the character's own | its own |

- **It sits on top of the voice state.** The expression owns the eyes' shape; the state keeps the gaze, the head turn, the blinks and the talking mouth. A sad Bean still turns to you while you talk.
- **Chirp and Hum** have no resting mouth to change (a beak, a grille), so theirs is in the eyes only.
- **Effects** still play on top, and an expression is never announced.
- In an FX Spec, `expression` can go in the file and in each state. The view's prop wins over the file's.

## Tap reaction

A tap or a click on a character plays `hop`: it crouches, hops and lands with a little squash, smiles, and glances toward where you tapped. The voice state carries on underneath.

- It's on by default on `SinuaCharacter` and `<sinua-character>`; the plain `SinuaView` leaves it off.
- A tap never cuts a running `success`, `error` or `celebrate`, and there are at most two hops a second.
- With reduced motion it only smiles.
- **Inside a tappable button or list row, set `tap: false`**, so the tap goes to your control and the character doesn't hop as well.

## Palette

`palette` repaints some of a character's colours and leaves the rest as drawn: `{ body: "#E63946" }`. To change only colours, this is all you need; no recipe.

- **Slots are named by the part they paint**, never by colour: `body` is every character's main colour, and `outline`, `cheeks` and `shine` mean the same everywhere. The eyes are `eyes` on a body face and `glow` on a screen face. An unknown slot is an error that suggests the closest name.
- **Tones follow.** Given `body`, its `bodyLight` and `bodyDark` move with it. A tone you give yourself wins.
- **After `hue`.** `hue` still turns the slots it turns; a colour you give is never turned.
- **The eyes stay readable.** When a new colour comes too close to the ink of the eyes and mouth, the ink moves to the far side, unless you set the ink too.
- **Labels for a colour picker:** every slot has a label and a one-line description in `spec/character-slot-labels.json`, with the localization key `sinua.slot.<character>.<slot>`.
- A change applies at once. In an FX Spec, `palette` can go in the file and in each state.

| Character | Slots (a slot marked +Light/Dark brings its tones) |
|---|---|
| `buzzy` | body (+Light/Dark), outline, screen, accent, glow, visor, visorEdge, shine |
| `hum` | body (+Light/Dark), stand (+Dark), grille, glow, outline, tallyListening, tallyThinking, tallyOff, shine |
| `wisp` | body, bodyMid, tail, outline, eyes, sparkles, twinkles, shine |
| `chirp` | body (+Light/Dark), belly, feathers, beak (+Dark), outline, eyes |
| `cuppa` | mug (+Light/Dark), coffee, crema, sleeve (+Dark), heart, shine, cheeks, saucer (+Dark), outline, eyes, steam |
| `bean` | body (+Light/Dark), groove, grooveEdge, shine, cheeks, aroma, foam, outline, eyes |
| `beep` | body (+Light/Dark), arms, screen, glow, accent, tallyOff, tallyListening, tallyThinking, outline, rivets, cheeks |

### Roles and the dark variant

A palette can also be written over **roles**, so one palette fits every character: each character maps `primary`, `secondary` and `accent` to its own slots.

- `{ primary: "#E63946" }` paints by role, without knowing the slot names. Slots you write win over roles.
- A role a character doesn't have is read as a slot name, so writing it is an error (Buzzy and Bean have no `secondary`).
- **Dark theme:** `{ primary: "#2E87C2", dark: { primary: "#2E6A9E" } }` gives your own dark variant. The view picks it when the theme is dark.

| Character | primary | secondary | accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `buzzy` | body | — | accent |
| `hum` | body | — | tallyListening |
| `wisp` | body | bodyMid | tail |
| `chirp` | body | belly | beak |
| `cuppa` | mug | sleeve | heart |
| `bean` | body | — | aroma |
| `beep` | body | arms | accent |

A character keeps its own colours otherwise: `color` and `gradient` don't apply, and in an [FX Spec](/docs/reference/fx-spec#111-characters) they are an error.

## The richer look

Airbrushed shading, rim light and film grain, with no blur: a character's soft `shade` masses and `rim` light give it depth, and `grain` lays a still, fine noise inside its body. The face stays crisp. `shading` turns the soft layers off and `grain` sets the amount; under low power both turn off.

## Options

| Prop | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `hue` | 232 | Turns the character's main colour. The eyes and the accents stay. |
| `palette` | none | Repaints colours by slot or role, with an optional `dark` variant (see [Palette](#palette)). |
| `expression` | none | A mood: `happy`, `surprised`, `thoughtful`, `sad`, `sleepy`. |
| `grain` | 0 | Film grain inside the body, 0 to 1. |
| `shading` | on | The soft shading and rim light. |
| `tap` | on | A tap plays a hop. Off inside a tappable control. |
| `mouth` | on | Off draws eyes only. |
| `accessories` | on | Off drops the extras: Buzzy's crest and ear arcs, Hum's tally light, Wisp's sparkles, Chirp's notes, Cuppa's steam. |
| `arms` | on | Beep's arms. |
| `look` | 1 | How much the eyes glance around on their own; `0` keeps them on the viewer. |
| `turn` | 0.71 | How far the head turns; `0` is flat. |
| `seed` | 0 | When it blinks and glances, so two characters side by side don't move in step. |

## Props

Every prop, per pattern. Besides the options above, the pose values the states set (`gazeX`, `lid`, `eyeSmile`, …) are props too, so you can tune how a state looks. Defaults can differ per size (20 · 32 · 64).
