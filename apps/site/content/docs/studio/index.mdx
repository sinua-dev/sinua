<Callout title="Studio">[Open the Studio](https://studio.sinua.dev), 14 days free, then $9 a month or $99 a year ([prices](/studio/)). Everything it makes can also be written by hand: see [From Studio to your app](/docs/getting-started/from-studio).</Callout>

The Studio has one tab per object: **Orbs, Signal, Ring, Beacon, Core, Character**. Each tab opens a grid of pattern thumbnails — static drawings, so the grid stays calm; the stage beside it is the live one.

![The Studio: the stage, the toolbar, the inspector and the export drawer](/studio/studio-tour.png)

Pick a pattern, then tune it:

- **Props:** every prop in the [catalog](/docs/catalog), grouped into **Appearance**, **Motion** and **Energy**, then **Color** and **Gradient**, then one group per [material](/docs/look/materials) — Glow, Noise, Particles, Liquid, Holographic, Pulse — and **Performance**. Each group keeps its rarely-used props behind **Advanced**, and reactive inputs have their own block. A slider only counts as a change when it differs from the pattern's default, so exports stay minimal.
- **Preview:** light and dark, sizes 20, 32 and 64, a pointer that scatters the dots, a live voice (microphone, test tone, a simulated conversation or a vendor session), and a cost badge.
- **Play effect:** the stage bar previews the [one-shot effects](/docs/connect/effects) (success, error, celebrate) on your design. Once you've played one, the exported code ends with how your app triggers it.
- **States and bindings:** see [Lifecycle & bindings](/docs/studio/lifecycle-and-bindings).
- **Transitions:** **Follow** plays a conversation and shows each state change as it happens, with how big the largest jump was; **Every change** shows all sixteen changes between the four voice states side by side.
- **Characters:** colours by slot, role or a named palette, eye styles, and the richer look. **Wardrobe** lists the cosmetics you can try on, from your file or a catalog pack.
- **Silhouette:** the orb's head and shoulders, plain or as a hologram, or your own outline pasted in and checked by the engine.
- **Export:** code for each platform, an FX Spec file, or a PNG or SVG image. See [Export](/docs/studio/export).
