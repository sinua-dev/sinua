Holographic recolours every element with an iridescent sweep that shifts with depth and with a slowly circling view point, like a foil card tilting in your hand. It keeps each element's lightness, so the pattern's shading survives.

## Cost and low power

Free: it only recolours. There's nothing to shed, so it isn't sheddable (and `disable: ["holographic"]` is an error that says so).

`speed` is turns per second of the sweep, in real time. Strokes sweep colour along their length.
