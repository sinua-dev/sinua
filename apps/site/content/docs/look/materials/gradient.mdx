A gradient colours elements by where they sit: a sweep from one hue to another (or three) across the frame. The full guide is on [Colour & theme](/docs/look/color-and-theme#a-gradient).

<Demo spec="spec/gradient.fxspec.json" />

## Cost and low power

No extra elements. Sheddable in low power (`disable: ["gradient"]`), which falls back to the pattern's plain colour.
